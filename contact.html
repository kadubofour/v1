Common questions

More answers about orders, payments and returns are in Customer help.

How long does delivery take?

24–48 hours within Ghana. Worldwide delivery takes 1–14 days depending on your address.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship to most countries. If yours isn't listed at checkout, send us a message and we'll arrange it.

How do I track my order?

Tracking is available for orders outside Accra. We send a text message at every stage of shipping.

How do I change my shipping address?

Send us your new address using the form on this page. Once a package has been picked up for delivery, the address can't be changed — please double-check it before you place your order.

What is your return and exchange policy?

Send a return request within 30 days of receiving your order. Wrongly processed orders and damaged products can be returned. We inspect damaged products to find where the damage happened. Authorised returns go to Kisseman Street, Accra, Ghana.

I received the wrong item

Send us a request for an exchange. We'll process and ship the correct order as soon as possible.

My order arrived damaged

Send a photo of the package as you received it via WhatsApp or any of our social pages. We'll review it and get back to you about an exchange.

Which products are right for my skin?

Message us with your concern and we'll suggest products, or book a facial for a full skin assessment.
