How we deliver, handle returns and payments, and look after your information.

Shipping and delivery

  • Within Ghana, orders arrive in 24–48 hours.
  • Outside Ghana, delivery takes 1–14 days depending on your address. We ship to most countries; if yours isn't listed at checkout, message us.
  • Items ordered together are shipped in one package, except for very large orders.
  • Tracking is available for orders outside Accra, and we text you at every stage of shipping. You can also track your order online.
  • Delivery fees are shown at checkout before you pay.

Changing your address

Send us your new address as quickly as possible. Once a package has been picked up for shipping, the address can't be changed, so please check it before placing your order.

Returns and exchanges

  • Send a return request within 30 days of receiving your order.
  • Wrongly processed orders and damaged products may be returned. We inspect damaged products to find out where the damage happened.
  • If you received the wrong item, request an exchange and we'll ship the correct order as soon as possible.
  • If your order arrived damaged, send a photo of the package as you received it through WhatsApp or our social media pages.
  • Authorised returns go to: Kisseman Street, Accra, Ghana.

Payments

  • Prices are in Ghana cedis (₵). No sales tax is charged.
  • Payments are protected with 128-bit SSL encryption. Your card details aren't available to us.
  • Submitted orders can't be changed or cancelled, so please check your products before you pay.
  • Pre-orders for sold-out items aren't available at the moment.

Privacy policy

We collect the details you give us when you order, create an account, subscribe or contact us: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address. We use them to process and deliver orders, send order updates, run reward points and, if you opt in, send skincare tips and offers.

We share delivery details only with the couriers who deliver your order and with our payment provider to process payments. We don't sell your information.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us, and you can unsubscribe from emails in My account.
