After looking at alternatives

Our journey began in 2021

After three years of retailing big skincare brands, we identified a gap: the desperate need for truly effective and affordable products made for natural, versatile African and African-descent skin. That is what Nyon Derma offers you.

In early 2021 we began the research behind what is now a life-transforming brand, committed to serving you in all things beauty.

A perfect fit for your everyday life

Designed to be versatile

Every product is made specially for you, taking into account the harsh climate of most African countries and the need to heal blemishes for healthy, glowing skin.

Unless otherwise stated, all our products are made for all skin types, all complexions, all genders and all races.

We're just getting started

Natural and scientific knowledge, blended

We are new and fresh. Our formulas pair botanicals like rose water, neem, turmeric, honey and papain with proven actives such as salicylic acid, niacinamide, vitamin C, glycolic acid, hyaluronic acid and urea.

Every product is tested and approved by the Food and Drugs Authority of the Republic of Ghana, and the active ingredients are listed on every label.
